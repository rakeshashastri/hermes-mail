Hermes Mail - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who this applies to

Hermes Mail is a personal tool used only by its owner, Rakesha Shastri. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Google gmail.readonly scope to read email in a single Gmail mailbox (rakesha.personaldata@gmail.com). That mailbox holds only mail that the owner forwards to it.

How data is used

Data is extracted from those emails and stored in a private database on the owner's own computer. It is used only to keep the owner's personal records up to date.

Sharing and storage

Data is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising. It is not used to train generalized AI models. The app's access token is stored only on the owner's computer.

Limits on access

The app cannot send, delete or change email. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/rakeshashastri/hermes-mail/issues.

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
